# Product brief: Casino Platform (suite)

All companies, studios, people and jurisdictions in this repository are invented.

## Problem
Casino operators cannot offer many studios' games to players across several regulated jurisdictions
without every integration re-building wallet, responsible-gambling, release and reporting rules,
because there is no shared, auditable platform between operators and game teams. It costs months per
integration, and every money or limit mistake puts a licence at risk.

One sentence per side:
- **Operators** can't launch a new studio's game in a new jurisdiction in days, because each game needs its own wallet integration, certification tracking and reports.
- **Game teams** can't ship a fix to all operators safely, because there is no single place to publish a build, roll it out gradually and kill it when it misbehaves.
- **Compliance** can't prove what happened to a player's money and limits, because the record is spread across systems that each keep a partial history.

## Users
| Who | Examples (invented) | How often | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operators (tenants) | Kestrel Gaming, Baobab Casino, Marula Bet, Fynbos Play | Continuous (API), daily (reports) | Offer games to their players, hold or mirror player balances, meet each jurisdiction's rules, reconcile money daily |
| Players (operators' customers) | ~1.2 M monthly active across operators | Sessions of 10–40 min | Play, see their balance, set limits, take a break or self-exclude |
| Game teams / studios | Ember Forge, Tidewater Studio, Quillon Games (studios); six platform teams | Weekly releases | Publish builds, track certification, roll out per operator, watch game health, roll back |
| Compliance officers | Operator and platform compliance | Daily, plus audits | Answer "what happened, who did it, under which rule" and file regulator reports on time |
| Platform engineers | Six teams of 4–7 (see `docs/team-topology.md`) | Daily | Change one service safely without breaking the others |

## Outcomes (targets for the first year)
- A new game on a new operator in a jurisdiction where it is certified: **under 2 days** (today: 6–10 weeks).
- **Zero** money discrepancies left unexplained at the daily reconciliation; every cent traceable to a ledger posting.
- **Zero** stakes accepted from excluded or over-limit players (measured by a daily audit query).
- Wallet API **p99 under 150 ms** at **2,000 bets/s** peak across all operators; **99.95%** availability.
- Regulator daily reports generated by **06:00** jurisdiction time, sealed with a checksum.
- Studio release to 10% canary in **under 1 hour**; kill switch effective in **under 5 seconds**.

## Constraints (regulated markets)
- **Licensing**: each jurisdiction (Jurisdiction A, B, C) has its own rule set: minimum age (18 or 21), minimum return-to-player, limit-increase cooling delay, reality-check interval, report formats and retention (5–7 years). Rule sets are versioned and never overlap in time.
- **Responsible gambling**: deposit, loss, wager and session-time limits; cool-off and self-exclusion that cannot be shortened; reality checks; a stake from an excluded player must be impossible, not just unlikely.
- **Money**: integer minor units only, double-entry and append-only, idempotent on the game server's transaction id; reconciliation with each operator daily.
- **Certification**: a build may only be served in a jurisdiction where a test lab certified that exact build.
- **Audit**: who did what, when, under which rule-set version, kept for the jurisdiction's retention period, tamper-evident.
- **Personal data** (GDPR/POPIA-style law): the platform keeps the minimum: an operator's player reference, jurisdiction, KYC outcome and an encrypted date of birth. Names, addresses and documents stay with the operator. Reporting uses keyed pseudonyms.
- **Data residency**: a jurisdiction may require its players' data in-region (drives the regional-cell scaling step).
- **Team**: six teams, one monorepo, independent deploys; AI agents write a share of the code, under governance (human review, critic review on money paths, prompt registry, eval gates).

## Out of scope (for now)
Payment processing (deposits from cards and banks stay with operators), sports betting, live-dealer video
streaming, player-facing UI (operators own the lobby), game maths certification itself (labs do it), a
cross-operator national self-exclusion register (a later integration, see DESIGN §11).

## Traits (for the architecture advisor), with evidence
- `SeveralBusinessAreas`: "wallet, responsible gambling, game release, bonuses, reporting and the developer portal each have their own language and owners."
- `MoneyMovement`: "every bet is a debit and a credit against a player balance; ~170 M wallet calls a day at peak season."
- `RegulatoryAudit`: "regulators ask for every limit change, exclusion, stake and payout, with the rule that applied, for 5–7 years."
- `PersonalData`: "player accounts, KYC status and date of birth are personal data."
- `MultiTenant`: "one deployment serves Kestrel Gaming, Baobab Casino, Marula Bet and Fynbos Play; none may ever see another's players or money."
- `ExternalPartnerIntegrations`: "studio game servers call the wallet; operators call launch and transfers; test labs send certificates; operators in pass-through mode expose their own wallet."
- `ComplexBusinessRules`: "per-jurisdiction limits, delays, exclusions, wagering requirements and certification gates."
- `GlobalScale`: "2,000 bets/s peak across operators in several regions, and the wallet's load is 100x the portal's."
